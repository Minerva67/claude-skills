# Worked Example — "Three Forces Against Your Estate" (gold standard)

Client brief: 62-year-old married advertising-agency founder, sold his firm in 2015
for $16M cash; 2015 married exemption $10.86M, 40% above; exemption sunsets ~half in
2026; goal = children first, estate tax second; recommendation = rolling GRAT.

Why this output won (director notes, learned across four failed drafts):

1. **Draft 1 failed** as a neutral GRAT-vs-CRAT explainer — informative, but nobody
   buys from a comparison lecture. Fix: client-as-hero, product enters late as relief.
2. **Draft 2 failed** as consumer-style emotion (warm family montage + scary IRS
   number + "let's make sure it's them"). An HNW buyer classifies that grammar as
   "annuity salesman" and raises shields. Fix: competence over emotion, respect,
   insight-reframe, quiet CTA.
3. **Draft 3 failed** as too austere — typography over silence, information-thin,
   kept circling "the children." Fix: restore the sales arc (anxiety → promise →
   mechanism → CTA) and give the atmosphere layer real jobs (erosion / shelter /
   legacy).
4. **Draft 4 failed** the absorption test — 35s of dense VO over one static mood
   shot; ears overloaded, eyes idle. Fix: THE ANCHOR RULE — every spoken fact fires
   a same-second on-screen event. This is the Dalio/Jarvis grammar and the single
   highest-leverage rule in the method.

The final prompt:

```
MASTER PROMPT — "Three Forces Against Your Estate" | Personalized HNW Advisory Film, ~300s
One film, six beats. Structure: anxiety → promise → mechanism → proof → verdict → CTA.

═══ PRIME DIRECTIVE: THE ANCHOR RULE ═══
Every fact the narrator speaks must trigger a synchronized on-screen event in the
same second — a line freezing, a curve climbing, a gap filling, a number rolling.
The visuals carry the details; the narration carries the big picture. During data
beats, no frame sits still longer than 4 seconds. Mood footage may breathe; data
may not.

═══ ENGINE ASSIGNMENT (applies to every beat) ═══
- PRECISION LAYER (the workhorse, ~70% of screen time): all charts, flows,
  formulas, timelines, counters, ledgers — rendered as crisp flat vector motion
  graphics. Monospaced digits so numbers roll and align. This layer is the
  protagonist; it must feel engineered, inevitable, alive.
- SPATIAL LAYER (used only where a physical object says it faster than words):
  minimal dimensional objects — a rising water level, a lever on a fulcrum, a
  stack of vaults. Never decorative; each one IS an argument.
- ATMOSPHERE LAYER (rare, expensive-feeling): soft filmic desaturated footage
  with subtle grain for mood and era — erosion tides, warm shelter light, one
  dignified legacy image. Always beneath the graphics, never touching a number.

═══ ART SYSTEM (one look, end to end) ═══
Ink-navy base, bone-white type, brass/gold as the single global accent.
Serif headlines; monospaced numerals. Two semantic colors carried through all
six beats: EROSION/LOSS = cold slate-red; PROTECTED/FAMILY = warm brass.
Every gain and every loss on screen wears its color — the viewer learns the
code in beat 1 and reads every later chart instantly. Atmosphere footage:
low-saturation, warm, 35mm grain. Unhurried, private-bank pacing throughout.

═══ FIXED FACTS (use exactly; personalization slots in braces) ═══
{CLIENT}=David, 62, married; sold his ad agency in 2015 for $16,000,000 cash.
{FAMILY}=two adult children. Exemption today: $10.86M (married), 40% tax above.
Exemption scheduled to fall ~half in 2026. Exposed today: $5.14M → ≈$2.06M tax.
IRS benchmark rate ≈2%. Worked example: $5,000,000 into the structure.
Assumed growth for projections: 6%/yr. Recommendation: a rolling GRAT.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BEAT 1 — THREE FORCES (50s) | why he must care — insight-anxiety, not fear
Atmosphere: a slow tide eating a shoreline, desaturated, beneath everything.
Precision layer builds ON TOP, one force per VO sentence:
  (1) a brass horizontal line labeled $10.86M draws in — then visibly FREEZES
      (frost texture, lock icon);
  (2) a white curve labeled "your $16M" enters below and climbs past it at 6%/yr;
      the area between curve and line fills cold slate-red as it grows;
  (3) at the 2026 tick, the frozen line ITSELF drops by half — the red area
      lurches wider; a monospaced counter in the red zone rolls up through
      $2,060,000 and keeps climbing as the timeline runs to his age 77.
VO: "David — three forces are working against your estate at once, and they
compound. One: your shield is frozen. $10.86 million exempt from a 40% estate
tax — fixed. Two: your money is not frozen. Sixteen million, growing — every
year more of it crosses that line into taxable territory. Three: in 2026 the
line itself is set to fall by half. Do nothing, and the cost isn't the two
million you see today. It's a gap that widens every year you're alive."

BEAT 2 — THE REVERSAL (35s) | the outcome he wants, asserted as real
Spatial layer: the red zone from beat 1 lifts off the chart, becomes a physical
red mass — then a gate opens and the mass DIVERTS mid-air, re-coloring to brass
as it lands in a vault labeled with {FAMILY}. Atmosphere beneath shifts from
tide to warm harbor light. Precision layer stamps three plain-language badges
as VO names them: "growth → family, gift-tax-free" / "≈$0 of exemption used" /
"reversible."
VO: "Now reverse it. The growth on that money passes to your children instead
of the IRS — gift-tax-free. Doing it consumes almost none of your frozen
exemption, so your shield stays intact. And if you ever change your mind, it
unwinds. This isn't a wish. It's a structure — and it's built for exactly
your moment."

BEAT 3 — THE MACHINE (60s) | how it works — pure precision, densest beat
No atmosphere. Full-screen vector machine, assembling as VO narrates:
  $5M flows into a chamber labeled GRAT → a pipe loops an annuity BACK to a
  node labeled YOU across a 2-year timeline (two payments tick off) → inside
  the chamber, a fulcrum: one side "what your assets earn," other side "IRS
  benchmark ≈2%" → everything above the 2% mark visibly SHEARS OFF as brass
  particles and streams to the {FAMILY} vault, tagged "outside your estate,
  gift-tax-free." A meter labeled "exemption spent" stays pinned at ≈$0.
VO: "Here's the machine. You place five million into the trust. Over two years
it pays that value back to you as an annuity — so in the IRS's math, you gave
away nothing, and the gift meter reads zero. But the IRS prices that math at
its own benchmark — about two percent. Every dollar your assets earn above
two percent doesn't come back to you. It shears off — to your children,
outside your estate, tax-free. The bar isn't beating the market. It's beating
two percent."

BEAT 4 — THE FLOOR AND THE ROLL (55s) | risk answered, then multiplied
Precision layer, two acts:
  (a) THE FLOOR: the beat-3 machine re-runs with a falling return line — the
      shear-off stream thins to zero, and the full $5M pipe simply returns to
      YOU; a caption stamps "downside: you get your money back."
  (b) THE ROLL: the timeline extends; a NEW trust chamber stacks on top each
      year — 2015, 2016, 2017… good years shear brass to the vault, flat years
      return quietly; the vault's monospaced total ratchets UP only. A slim
      sand-timer beside the stack marks the only condition: "outlive each
      2-year term" — at 62, it drains without drama.
VO: "And if the assets have a bad two years? Nothing shears off — the money
simply comes back to you. You've lost legal fees and nothing else. That's the
floor. So you don't do this once. You launch a new one every year. Every
strong year locks its gains into your children's vault, permanently. Every
flat year costs you nothing. Heads you win, tails you break even — repeated
annually. The only requirement: outlive each two-year term. At sixty-two,
that's the easiest term on the sheet."

BEAT 5 — THE LEDGER (60s) | the payoff — his two futures, side by side
Split screen, both halves running the SAME 15-year timeline simultaneously:
  LEFT "Do nothing": the beat-1 chart replays compressed — red gap widening,
  IRS row rolling up past $2M toward $4M+ as the estate grows and the 2026
  cliff hits. LEFT is lit cold.
  RIGHT "The rolling structure": the beat-4 stack replays compressed — brass
  ratcheting into the family vault year by year, IRS row nearly flat, exemption
  meter still full. RIGHT is lit warm.
  At second 45 both freeze; one final row slams in across both halves —
  "difference to your children:" and a monospaced figure rolls up and HOLDS.
VO: "Run both futures forward fifteen years. On the left, nothing changes:
your estate compounds, the exemption falls, and the taxable gap more than
doubles — the check to the IRS grows every year you live. On the right, the
same growth, the same you — but each year's upside has already left the
estate and landed with your children, and your exemption is still intact.
Same life. Same money. The only difference is which side of the line the
growth landed on — and that difference is measured in millions."

BEAT 6 — LEGACY + CTA (40s) | one earned image, then the door
Atmosphere: ONE dignified, restrained image — a hand passing a worn key across
a desk, warm light, 2015-film texture. No montage. Precision layer overlays
three small monospaced lines, personalized: "modeled on your numbers" /
"trust size, payout, and what reaches your children — already computed" /
firm mark, small, brass. Silence before the last VO line.
VO: "You spent thirty years building it. The next two years decide how much
of it stays built. We've already run this on your actual numbers — the trust
size, the annuity, the figure that reaches your children. The analysis is
ready whenever you are. {ADVISOR}, {FIRM}."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BUDGET: 50+35+60+55+60+40 = 300s. Precision layer leads beats 1,3,4,5;
spatial metaphors only in 2 (diversion) and 4 (stack, timer); atmosphere only
in 1 (erosion), 2 (shelter), 6 (legacy). Recurring devices — the frozen line,
the red gap, the brass vault, the shear-off stream — are introduced once and
reused, so the film teaches its own visual language and each beat reads faster
than the last.
```

Transferable devices worth reusing in new domains (rename, don't copy):
- **The frozen line vs. the growing curve** — any "static protection vs. compounding
  exposure" problem (insurance coverage, compliance debt, concentration risk).
- **The shear-off stream** — any mechanism where value above a threshold routes to a
  different destination (carried interest, tax arbitrage, overflow savings).
- **The floor re-run** — answer downside risk by literally re-running the machine
  with bad inputs and showing nothing breaks.
- **The ratcheting stack** — any repeatable annual/quarterly structure.
- **The two-futures ledger** — universal payoff beat; always the longest beat.
- **One earned image** — the single atmosphere shot at the end; more is montage,
  and montage reads as advertising.
